🚀 I just open-sourced **SangamPay**: a self-hosted payment platform for India and the world.

Every business I know juggles multiple gateways, pays MDR even on UPI, waits T+2 for settlements, and loses sales
whenever their one gateway goes down.

So I built the layer I wanted: one API, one checkout, one dashboard, sitting on top of the gateways you already
use, with your money landing straight in your own accounts.

What's inside 👇

💳 UPI (including **zero-fee direct UPI** to your own UPI ID), net banking, cards, ACH/SEPA and PayPal
🔁 Subscriptions with **UPI AutoPay** and **e-NACH**, trials and smart dunning
🧾 **GST tax invoices** (CGST/SGST/IGST, HSN/SAC) with a built-in Pay button
🔗 Payment links and printable QR codes
🧠 **Smart routing** by live success rate or lowest cost, with automatic failover when a gateway goes down
🏦 Bank-statement **auto-reconciliation** for UPI payments
💸 Payouts (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS/UPI) and marketplace split payments
🛡️ Fraud rules with risk scores, an AES-256 encrypted vault, signed webhooks, team roles and an audit log
👩‍💻 Python and Node SDKs, a WooCommerce plugin and a Shopify bridge

It's **non-custodial**: SangamPay never touches the money, so anyone can self-host it. MIT licensed.

Sangam (संगम) means "confluence", where rivers meet. Here, every payment rail meets in one place.

⭐ GitHub: https://github.com/Rohesen/sangampay

I'd love feedback, stars, and contributors, especially for new gateway adapters (Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe PG…).
