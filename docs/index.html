Features

Everything a growing business needs

The parts that usually take five different products, in one self-hosted platform.

Accept every payment

UPI (QR and intent), net banking, cards, ACH, SEPA and PayPal on a hosted, mobile-first checkout.

Subscriptions

UPI AutoPay, e-NACH and card mandates, or an invoice each cycle. Trials, quantities and smart dunning retries.

GST tax invoices

CGST + SGST or IGST chosen automatically from place of supply, with HSN/SAC codes and a Pay button.

Smart routing

Route by priority, live success rate or lowest cost. If a gateway goes down, the next one takes over automatically.

Auto-reconciliation

Upload your bank statement and zero-fee UPI payments are matched by UTR and confirmed for you.

Payouts and splits

Pay vendors by IMPS, NEFT, RTGS or UPI, and split marketplace payments between sellers automatically.

Payment links and QR

Sell without a website. Share a link or print a QR for your counter, with fixed or pay-what-you-want amounts.

Fraud and security

Risk scores, blocklists, velocity limits, an AES-256 encrypted vault, signed webhooks and an audit log.

One ledger, real analytics

Volume, success rates by method and gateway, MRR, churn and exports across every gateway, in one place.

Checkout

A checkout your customers will trust

Fast, mobile-first and in dark mode automatically. Customers pick UPI, a bank or a card. Bank logins and card numbers are only ever typed on the bank's own page.

  • ✓Zero-fee UPI: QR code, one-tap app link and UTR confirmation.
  • ✓Every method your connected gateways support, on one page.
  • ✓Embeds anywhere with one script tag or a plain redirect.
Billing

GST invoices and subscriptions on autopilot

Bill once or every month. SangamPay works out the tax, collects through mandates and chases failed payments for you.

  • ✓Tax invoices with CGST/SGST or IGST, HSN/SAC and your GSTIN.
  • ✓UPI AutoPay and e-NACH: customers approve once, renewals just happen.
  • ✓Smart dunning retries failed renewals on your schedule.
Developers

An API you'll enjoy

Clean REST, idempotency keys, signed webhooks, OpenAPI docs, a sandbox with time travel, and zero-dependency SDKs.

  • ✓Python and Node SDKs with retries and webhook verification.
  • ✓WooCommerce plugin and a Shopify bridge.
  • ✓Team keys with roles and a full audit log.

Get started

Running in two minutes

Everything works in test mode out of the box, with a built-in sandbox for every payment method.

Clone and run

Python 3.10+, SQLite by default.

git clone https://github.com/Rohesen/sangampay
cd sangampay
pip install -r requirements.txt
uvicorn sangam.main:app

Load demo data

Realistic payments, subscriptions and invoices.

python -m sangam.demo
# prints a test key → open
# localhost:8000/dashboard

Go live

Docker + Postgres, then connect your own gateway accounts.

cp .env.example .env
docker compose up -d

Non-custodial by design. SangamPay never receives or holds customer funds. Payments settle in your own Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal or bank accounts, which is why anyone can self-host it. Keep it that way: pooling funds for others requires a payment aggregator licence (RBI in India).
